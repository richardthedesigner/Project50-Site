01 / Who we are

Strength is infrastructure, not spectacle.

Project 50 exists in opposition to disposable fitness culture. We don't sell transformations. We build systems, physical, mental, structural, designed to compound over years, not collapse after twelve weeks.

We sit between elite sport discipline, academic rigour, and inclusive coaching. Think old athletics clubs. Chalk boards. Timetables. Track geometry. Library quiet. Evening light on court.

Est. for decades

02 / Founder

Built on evidence. Driven by care.

Jo brings together elite sport coaching, academic credibility, and an unwavering commitment to every person she works with. From children and young people to older adults building strength and longevity.

With roots in Cheltenham Ladies' College and university-level lecturing at the University of Gloucestershire, her practice is grounded in research and refined through years of real-world coaching. Safety, professionalism, and evidence-led programming are the foundation, never extras.

Project 50 is the platform that brings this work together: structured, intelligent, and built to last.
