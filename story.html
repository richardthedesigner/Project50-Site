01 / The question

What would coaching look like if it were designed to last a lifetime?

Project 50 started with that question. Everything since has been an answer to it.

02 / Meet Jo Hucker

From the court to the platform.

I started Project 50 because I saw a need for something clearer than another training plan. More structured. More supportive.

With years of coaching in schools, clinics, and community spaces, I began refining an approach that could work across real lives and real time. A system rather than a bootcamp. One that grows with you and holds its shape under pressure.

My work draws from elite institutions like Cheltenham Ladies' College and university-level lecturing at the University of Gloucestershire, environments where performance, safety, and excellence are non-negotiable.

I work with children, adults rebuilding their strength, and older adults extending their capability. Every body is coached with care and adaptability.

Three pillars. One standard.

04 / Philosophy

Built to compound.

Project 50 exists in opposition to disposable fitness culture. We build systems designed to compound over years, not collapse after twelve weeks.

The number 50 signals longevity: decades of capability, sustained commitment, and the quiet discipline of showing up when no one is watching.

Many adults feel disconnected from their former athletic identity. Project 50 provides a structured path back, toward capability rather than youth.

Strength is infrastructure, not spectacle.
