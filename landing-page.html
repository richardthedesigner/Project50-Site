Who we are

Strength is infrastructure, not spectacle.

Est. for decades

Project 50 exists in opposition to disposable fitness culture. We don't sell transformations. We build systems, physical, mental, structural, designed to compound over years, not collapse after twelve weeks.

The number 50 is not a programme length. It signals longevity: decades of capability, sustained commitment, and the quiet discipline of showing up when no one is watching.

We sit between elite sport discipline, academic rigour, and inclusive coaching. Think old athletics clubs. Chalk boards. Timetables. Track geometry. Library quiet. Evening light on court.

Founder

Meet Jo

Built on evidence. Driven by care.

Jo brings together elite sport coaching, academic credibility, and an unwavering commitment to every person she works with. from children and young people to older adults navigating strength and longevity.

With roots in Cheltenham Ladies' College and university-level lecturing at the University of Gloucestershire, her practice is grounded in research and refined through years of real world coaching. Safety, professionalism, and evidence led programming are not extras. They are the foundation.

Project 50 is the platform that brings this work together: structured, intelligent, and built to last.
