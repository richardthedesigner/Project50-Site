Privacy policy.

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Who we are

Project 50 is a strength and longevity coaching practice based in Cheltenham, Gloucestershire, United Kingdom. For anything related to your data, contact us at hello@project50.co.uk.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect your name, email address, the programme you're interested in, and your message. When you subscribe to the newsletter we collect your email address. We don't collect anything else through this site, and we never buy or sell personal data.

How your data is processed

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-processing service. Web3Forms forwards your submission by email and does not use your data for its own purposes. You can read their policy at web3forms.com/privacy.

Why we're allowed to process it

We process enquiry data on the basis of legitimate interest: you contacted us and we need your details to reply. Newsletter signups are processed on the basis of consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us.

Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics that run without cookies and without advertising trackers. We see aggregate page views and general referrer information, never your identity.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any coaching relationship that follows. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email hello@project50.co.uk and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
